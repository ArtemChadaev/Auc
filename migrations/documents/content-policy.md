# Content Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Introduction

This Content Policy establishes the standards and guidelines for all content published on the **Auc** digital auction platform. It applies to every form of user-generated content, including but not limited to:

- Lot listings and descriptions
- Uploaded images and thumbnails
- Usernames and display names
- Avatars and profile images
- Comments, messages, and any other text submitted to the platform

All users are expected to comply with this policy. By using Auc, you agree to abide by these standards. Auc reserves the right to enforce this policy at its sole discretion.

> **Note:** Auc is a digital auction platform that operates exclusively with virtual (non-real) currency. All items listed and traded on the platform are digital. Auc bears no financial responsibility for any transactions conducted on the platform.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on Auc. This list is non-exhaustive; Auc may determine at its sole discretion that content not explicitly listed here still violates the spirit of this policy.

### 2.1 Illegal Content

Any content that violates applicable laws or regulations is prohibited. This includes content that is illegal to produce, distribute, or possess in any jurisdiction.

### 2.2 Pornographic, Sexually Explicit, or NSFW Material

Content that is pornographic, sexually explicit, or otherwise not safe for work (NSFW) is not permitted. This includes nudity, sexual acts, and sexually suggestive material in any form — images, descriptions, or text.

### 2.3 Hate Speech, Discrimination, and Harassment

Content that promotes, incites, or glorifies hatred, violence, or discrimination against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic is prohibited. Harassment, bullying, and targeted abuse of other users are not tolerated.

### 2.4 Violence, Gore, and Graphic Content

Content depicting extreme violence, gore, mutilation, or other graphic material intended to shock, disturb, or offend is not permitted.

### 2.5 Content Promoting Illegal Activities

Content that encourages, instructs, or facilitates illegal activities — including but not limited to drug use, weapons trafficking, fraud, or money laundering — is prohibited.

### 2.6 Malware, Viruses, and Harmful Software

Uploading, distributing, or linking to malware, viruses, trojans, ransomware, spyware, or any other software designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to systems is strictly prohibited.

### 2.7 Spam, Scam, and Phishing Content

Content designed to deceive users, solicit personal information under false pretenses, or flood the platform with repetitive, unsolicited, or irrelevant material is prohibited. This includes phishing attempts, fraudulent schemes, and any form of spam.

### 2.8 Intellectual Property Infringement

Content that infringes on the intellectual property rights of others — including copyrights, trademarks, patents, and trade secrets — is not permitted. Users must have the legal right to share any content they upload.

### 2.9 Misleading or Fraudulent Lot Descriptions

Lot descriptions must accurately represent the digital item being offered. Intentionally misleading, deceptive, or fraudulent descriptions designed to misrepresent the nature, quality, or characteristics of a lot are prohibited.

### 2.10 Personal Information of Others (Doxxing)

Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private or personally identifiable information — such as real names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, financial information, or identification documents — without their explicit consent is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Lot Listing Standards

All lot listings on Auc must adhere to the following standards:

- **Accurate Descriptions** — Every lot must include a truthful and accurate description of the digital item being offered. Descriptions must not exaggerate, misrepresent, or omit material details about the item.
- **Relevant Images** — All images attached to a lot listing must be directly relevant to and representative of the digital item being sold.
- **No Misleading Thumbnails or Images** — Thumbnails and preview images must not be deceptive or unrelated to the actual lot content. Clickbait imagery is not permitted.
- **Appropriate Category Selection** — Lots must be listed under the most appropriate and accurate category. Intentional miscategorization to gain visibility or mislead buyers is prohibited.
- **Digital Items Only** — All lots listed on Auc must be digital items. Physical goods, services, and non-digital assets may not be listed on the platform.

---

## 4. Image Requirements

All images uploaded to Auc — including lot images, thumbnails, avatars, and profile pictures — must meet the following requirements:

- **Appropriate for All Audiences** — Images must be suitable for a general audience. Content that is explicit, graphic, or otherwise inappropriate is not permitted.
- **No Third-Party Watermarks** — Images must not contain watermarks, logos, or branding from other platforms, services, or individuals, unless they are part of the original digital item being listed.
- **Rights to Upload** — Users must have the legal right to upload and distribute any images they submit to the platform. Unauthorized use of copyrighted or proprietary images is prohibited.
- **Platform Removal Rights** — Auc reserves the right to remove, modify, or restrict any image that violates this policy at any time and without prior notice to the user.

---

## 5. Username and Avatar Policy

- **Usernames** must not contain offensive, discriminatory, hateful, sexually explicit, or otherwise inappropriate language. Usernames that impersonate other users, public figures, or Auc staff are prohibited.
- **Avatars** must comply with all image requirements outlined in Section 4. Avatars that are offensive, explicit, or violate any part of this Content Policy will be removed, and the user may face enforcement actions.

Auc reserves the right to force-reset any username or avatar that violates this policy without prior notice.

---

## 6. Enforcement

Auc takes content policy violations seriously and enforces this policy through the following measures:

### 6.1 Content Removal

Auc may remove, hide, or restrict any content — including lot listings, images, descriptions, usernames, and avatars — that violates this policy **at any time and without prior notice**.

### 6.2 Lot and Image Deletion

Lots and associated images that do not comply with this Content Policy may be deleted without warning. Auc is under no obligation to preserve or restore removed content.

### 6.3 Disciplinary Progression

Enforcement actions follow a progressive discipline model, though Auc reserves the right to skip steps for severe violations:

1. **Warning** — A formal notice is issued to the user identifying the violation and requiring compliance.
2. **Temporary Suspension** — The user's account is temporarily suspended for a defined period. During suspension, the user cannot list lots, bid, or interact with the platform.
3. **Permanent Ban** — The user's account is permanently terminated, and they are barred from creating new accounts.

### 6.4 Repeat Violations

Users who repeatedly violate this Content Policy will face escalated enforcement actions, up to and including permanent account termination. A pattern of violations demonstrates a disregard for platform standards and will be treated accordingly.

---

## 7. Reporting

Users are encouraged to report any content they believe violates this Content Policy. Reports can be submitted through the platform's built-in reporting tools or by contacting the content moderation team directly.

- All reports will be reviewed by the Auc moderation team.
- Reports are treated confidentially; the identity of the reporter will not be disclosed to the reported user.
- Auc will make reasonable efforts to review and act on reports in a timely manner, but does not guarantee specific response times.
- Submitting false or malicious reports may itself constitute a violation of this policy.

---

## 8. Appeals

Users whose content has been removed or whose accounts have been subject to enforcement actions may appeal the decision.

- Appeals must be submitted in writing to the content moderation team at the contact address listed below.
- Appeals should include a clear explanation of why the user believes the decision was made in error.
- Auc will review appeals and respond within a reasonable timeframe.
- The decision made upon appeal review is final.
