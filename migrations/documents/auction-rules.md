# Auction Rules

**Platform:** Auc — Digital Auction Platform  
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Introduction

These Auction Rules ("Rules") govern all auctions conducted on the Auc platform. By creating an auction, listing a lot, or placing a bid, you agree to abide by these Rules in their entirety. These Rules supplement the platform's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

All participants — whether auction creators, lot sellers, or bidders — are expected to act in good faith and comply with these Rules at all times.

---

## 2. Virtual Currency Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All prices, bids, balances, and transactions on Auc are denominated in **virtual (fake) currency** that holds **no real-world monetary value**.

- **No real money** is exchanged at any point during the auction process.
- Virtual currency **cannot be converted, redeemed, or exchanged** for real money, goods, or services outside the platform.
- The platform bears **no financial responsibility** for any virtual currency balances, transactions, or losses.
- Virtual currency balances exist solely for the purpose of participating in platform activities and may be adjusted, reset, or revoked by the platform at any time.

## 3. Creating Auctions

### 3.1 Eligibility

Any registered user in good standing may create an auction on the platform.

### 3.2 Required Information

When creating an auction, the creator must provide:

- **Title** — A clear and descriptive name for the auction.
- **Description** — A detailed explanation of the auction's purpose or theme.
- **Start Time** — The date and time when the auction opens for bidding.
- **End Time** — The date and time when the auction closes.

### 3.3 Duration

Auctions have a **fixed duration** defined by the start and end times set at creation. Once an auction is live, the end time cannot be extended by the creator.

### 3.4 Creator Responsibility

The auction creator is fully responsible for:

- The accuracy of the auction's title and description.
- Ensuring the auction complies with all platform policies.
- Managing the lots listed within their auction.

---

## 4. Listing Lots

### 4.1 Adding Lots

Lots are individual items listed within an auction and made available for bidding. Users may add lots to auctions they have created.

### 4.2 Required Lot Information

Each lot must include the following:

| Field             | Requirement                                      |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **Title**         | A clear, accurate name for the item              |
| **Description**   | A detailed description of the digital item       |
| **Category**      | An appropriate category selected from the list   |
| **Image(s)**      | At least one image representing the item         |
| **Start Price**   | The minimum bid amount (opening price)           |
| **Minimum Step**  | The minimum bid increment above the current bid  |

### 4.3 Digital Items Only

All lots listed on Auc must be **digital items**. Physical goods, real-world services, and items requiring off-platform delivery are not permitted.

### 4.4 Ownership & Removal

- Lots belong to their creator.
- The platform reserves the right to **remove any lot** that violates platform policies, these Rules, or applicable guidelines — without prior notice.

---

## 5. Bidding Rules

### 5.1 Eligibility

Only **registered users** with an active account may place bids on lots.

### 5.2 Bid Requirements

- The **first bid** on any lot must be **equal to or greater than** the lot's start price.
- Each subsequent bid must be **at least** the current highest bid **plus** the minimum bid step.
- Bids must be placed in whole units of the platform's virtual currency, unless otherwise specified.

### 5.3 Finality of Bids

> [!CAUTION]
> **All bids are final.** Once a bid is placed, it **cannot be retracted, cancelled, or modified** under any circumstances.

By placing a bid, the user commits to purchasing the lot at the bid amount if no higher bid is received before the auction ends.

### 5.4 Active Period

Bidding is only permitted during the auction's **active period** — that is, between the auction's designated start time and end time. Bids submitted outside this window will be rejected.

---

## 6. Winning & Completion

### 6.1 Determining the Winner

When an auction's end time is reached, the **highest bidder** on each lot at the moment of closing is declared the winner of that lot.

### 6.2 Unsold Lots

If **no bids** are placed on a lot during the auction's active period, the lot is marked as **unsold** and remains with its creator.

### 6.3 Virtual Currency Deduction

Upon winning a lot, the winning bid amount in virtual currency is **automatically deducted** from the winner's balance. If the winner's balance is insufficient at the time of deduction, the platform may void the win and take appropriate action.

---

## 7. Fair Play

All users are expected to participate in auctions honestly and in good faith. The following activities are **strictly prohibited**:

### 7.1 Shill Bidding

Bidding on lots that you yourself have listed, or arranging for others to bid on your lots to artificially inflate prices, is prohibited.

### 7.2 Multiple Account Manipulation

Creating or using multiple accounts to manipulate bidding outcomes, circumvent restrictions, or gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.

### 7.3 Collusion

Any agreement or coordination between bidders to suppress competition, fix prices, or manipulate auction results is prohibited.

> [!WARNING]
> Violations of the Fair Play rules may result in immediate account suspension, voiding of bids, forfeiture of virtual currency, or permanent banning from the platform.
