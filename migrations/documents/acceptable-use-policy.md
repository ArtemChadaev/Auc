# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**
**Version: 1.0**

---

## 1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the standards of behavior expected from all users of the Auc digital auction platform ("Auc," "the Platform," "we," or "us"). By accessing or using Auc, you agree to comply with this policy in its entirety.

Auc is a digital auction platform where all items are digital and all transactions are conducted using virtual (non-real) currency. This policy exists to ensure a fair, safe, and enjoyable experience for every participant. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including permanent removal from the Platform.

This AUP supplements the Platform's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and any other applicable policies. In the event of a conflict, the Terms of Service shall take precedence.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use

Users are expected to engage with the Platform in a lawful, respectful, and good-faith manner. The following behaviors are encouraged and considered acceptable:

### 2.1 Good-Faith Participation in Auctions

Users may create, list, and participate in auctions provided they do so honestly and with genuine intent. Sellers should intend to deliver the listed digital item upon completion of a sale, and buyers should intend to follow through on winning bids.

### 2.2 Accurate Item Listings

All digital items listed for auction must be described truthfully and accurately. Listings should include:

- A clear and honest description of the digital item.
- Accurate representations of the item's condition, features, and any limitations.
- Appropriate categorization and tagging.

Misleading, deceptive, or materially inaccurate listings are not permitted.

### 2.3 Honest Bidding

Users must bid honestly and with the genuine intention of completing the transaction if they win. Bids placed on the Platform represent a commitment to pay the bid amount in virtual currency should the user win the auction.

### 2.4 Respectful Communication

All interactions between users — whether in auction comments, messages, forums, or any other communication channel provided by the Platform — must remain civil, respectful, and constructive. Users should treat others with courtesy and professionalism.

### 2.5 Reporting Violations

Users are encouraged to report any activity they believe violates this policy, the Terms of Service, or any applicable rules. Timely reporting helps maintain the integrity of the Platform and protects the community. Reports can be submitted through the Platform's reporting tools or by contacting us directly (see [Section 7](#7-contact)).

## 3. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on the Platform. This list is not exhaustive; Auc reserves the right to determine, at its sole discretion, whether any conduct violates the spirit of this policy.

### 3.1 Hacking and Exploitation

Attempting to exploit, hack, reverse-engineer, or otherwise compromise the security, integrity, or availability of the Platform, its infrastructure, or any associated systems is strictly forbidden. This includes, but is not limited to, unauthorized access to accounts, servers, databases, or networks.

### 3.2 Unauthorized Automation

Using automated tools, bots, scripts, crawlers, or any other automated means to interact with the Platform without prior written permission from Auc is prohibited. This includes automated bidding, listing, account creation, and data collection.

### 3.3 Multi-Accounting and Ban Evasion

Creating or operating multiple accounts for the purpose of circumventing bans, suspensions, or restrictions, or for manipulating auctions, virtual currency balances, or any other Platform feature is prohibited. Each user is permitted one active account unless explicitly authorized otherwise.

### 3.4 Shill Bidding and Bid Manipulation

Shill bidding — placing bids on your own auctions or coordinating with others to artificially inflate prices — is strictly prohibited. Any form of bid manipulation, including bid retraction abuse, last-moment bid coordination schemes, or collusion between bidders, is forbidden.

### 3.5 Harassment and Abuse

Harassing, threatening, bullying, intimidating, or abusing other users in any form is not tolerated. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Personal attacks, hate speech, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated unwanted contact or stalking behavior.
- Doxxing or sharing another user's personal information without consent.
- Sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise offensive content directed at other users.

### 3.6 Impersonation

Impersonating another user, Platform staff, administrator, or any other individual or entity — whether through usernames, profile information, communications, or any other means — is prohibited.

### 3.7 Real-Money Transactions

Attempting to conduct, facilitate, or solicit real-money transactions through the Platform is strictly forbidden. Auc operates exclusively with virtual currency that holds no real-world monetary value. Users must not attempt to buy, sell, trade, or exchange virtual currency or digital items for real money, cryptocurrency, or any other real-world consideration.

### 3.8 Scraping and Data Mining

Scraping, data mining, harvesting, or otherwise systematically extracting content, data, or information from the Platform through any means — automated or manual — without prior written authorization is prohibited.

### 3.9 Interference with Other Users

Intentionally interfering with, disrupting, or degrading another user's ability to use and enjoy the Platform is not permitted. This includes spamming, flooding, or engaging in any behavior designed to obstruct or diminish the experience of others.

### 3.10 Bug and Vulnerability Exploitation

Exploiting bugs, glitches, vulnerabilities, or errors in the Platform for personal gain or to the detriment of others is prohibited. Users who discover such issues are expected to report them promptly and responsibly to the Auc team (see [Section 7](#7-contact)) rather than exploiting them. Responsible disclosure is appreciated and may be recognized by the Platform.

### 3.11 Attacks on Platform Infrastructure

Conducting or participating in Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, Denial of Service (DoS) attacks, or any other form of cyberattack targeting the Platform's infrastructure, servers, networks, or services is strictly prohibited. This extends to any action intended to degrade, disrupt, or disable the Platform's operations.

## 4. API Usage

If Auc provides access to an Application Programming Interface (API), the following rules apply:

- **Rate Limits**: Users must respect all published rate limits. Exceeding rate limits may result in temporary or permanent revocation of API access.
- **Authentication**: API access must use only authorized credentials. Sharing, leaking, or misusing API keys or tokens is prohibited.
- **Permitted Use**: The API may only be used for purposes consistent with this AUP and the Platform's Terms of Service. Unauthorized scraping, data harvesting, or automation through the API is prohibited.
- **Guidelines**: Users must follow all API documentation, guidelines, and best practices published by Auc. The Platform reserves the right to modify, restrict, or discontinue API access at any time, with or without notice.
- **No Abuse**: Any use of the API that places an unreasonable burden on Platform infrastructure or is designed to circumvent Platform rules is prohibited.

## 5. Consequences

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy will be addressed through a graduated enforcement process, as outlined below. Auc reserves the right to determine the appropriate response at its sole discretion based on the nature and severity of the violation.

### 5.1 Graduated Enforcement

| Stage | Action | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **1** | **Warning** | A formal notice is issued to the user identifying the violation and requesting immediate compliance. |
| **2** | **Temporary Suspension** | The user's account is temporarily suspended for a defined period. Access to auctions, bidding, listing, and communication features may be restricted. |
| **3** | **Permanent Ban** | The user's account is permanently terminated and the user is prohibited from creating new accounts on the Platform. |

### 5.2 Immediate Action

Severe violations — including but not limited to hacking, DDoS attacks, exploitation of vulnerabilities, real-money transactions, and serious harassment — may result in an **immediate permanent ban** without prior warning or temporary suspension.

### 5.3 Additional Measures

Auc may also take any of the following actions as appropriate:

- Removal or modification of offending listings, bids, or content.
- Forfeiture of virtual currency balances associated with the violation.
- Restriction of specific features or privileges.
- Reporting the activity to relevant authorities where applicable.

### 5.4 Appeals

Users who believe an enforcement action was taken in error may submit an appeal by contacting us at the address provided in [Section 7](#7-contact). Appeals will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, and the Platform's decision following review shall be final.

## 6. Reporting

If you witness or become aware of any activity that violates this Acceptable Use Policy, we encourage you to report it promptly. Reports help us maintain a fair and safe environment for all users.

### How to Report

- **In-Platform Reporting**: Use the built-in reporting tools available on listings, user profiles, and communication channels to flag violations directly.
- **Email**: Send a detailed report to **abuse@auc.example.com** (see [Section 7](#7-contact)).

### What to Include

When submitting a report, please provide as much detail as possible, including:

- The username or account involved in the violation.
- A description of the prohibited activity.
- Relevant screenshots, links, or other supporting evidence.
- The date and time of the incident.

### Confidentiality

All reports are treated confidentially. Auc will not disclose the identity of the reporter to the accused party except where required to do so for legitimate investigative or legal purposes.

### No Retaliation

Auc strictly prohibits retaliation against users who report violations in good faith. Any user found to be retaliating against a reporter will be subject to enforcement action under this policy.
