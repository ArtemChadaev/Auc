## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Auc** ("we," "us," "our," or the "Platform"). Auc is a digital auction platform where users can create auctions, list digital items (lots), and place bids using virtual (non-real) currency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you access or use the Auc platform, including our website, APIs, and any related services (collectively, the "Service").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently. By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

> **Important:** Auc operates exclusively with virtual currency. We do **not** process, store, or collect any real financial information such as credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials. All currency on the platform is fictional and holds no real-world monetary value.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide, maintain, and improve the Service.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you register for an Auc account, we collect:

- **Username** — A unique identifier you choose to represent yourself on the platform.
- **Email Address** — Used for account verification, authentication, and communication.
- **Password** — Stored exclusively in a securely hashed format. We never store passwords in plain text and cannot retrieve your original password.

### 2.2 Profile Information

You may optionally provide additional profile information, including:

- **Avatar Image** — A profile picture you upload to personalize your account. This image is stored in our cloud storage infrastructure.

All profile information beyond the required account details is voluntary. You can use the platform without providing optional profile data.

### 2.3 Activity Data

As you interact with the platform, we automatically record information about your usage, including:

- **Auctions Created** — Records of auctions you have initiated, including auction titles, descriptions, settings, and timestamps.
- **Lots Listed** — Details of digital items you have listed for auction, including item descriptions and associated metadata.
- **Bids Placed** — A history of bids you have submitted on lots, including bid amounts (in virtual currency) and timestamps.
- **Transaction History** — Records of virtual currency transfers, wins, and other platform-related transactions.

### 2.4 Technical Data

When you access the Service, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- **IP Address** — Your Internet Protocol address, used for security monitoring and approximate geolocation.
- **Browser Information** — Browser type, version, and language preferences.
- **Device Information** — Operating system, device type, screen resolution, and unique device identifiers.
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies** — Small data files stored on your device to maintain session state and preferences (see Section 8 for more details).
- **Access Logs** — Timestamps of access, pages visited, features used, and referral URLs.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Infrastructure

- **Account and Activity Data** — Stored in secured databases with access controls and encryption at rest.
- **Passwords** — All user passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard algorithms before storage. We do not store plain-text passwords at any point.
- **Images** — Avatar images and any other user-uploaded media are stored in S3-compatible cloud storage with appropriate access policies.

### 4.2 Security Measures

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS).
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest.
- Access controls limiting who can access user data internally.
- Regular security assessments and monitoring.
- Secure password hashing with salting.

### 4.3 No Absolute Guarantee

While we strive to protect your personal information, **no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure**. We cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials, and you should notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access to your account.

## 8. Cookies

### 8.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you access the Service. They help us recognize your browser, maintain your session, and understand how you use the platform.

### 8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

- **Session Cookies (Essential)** — These cookies are strictly necessary for authentication and maintaining your logged-in state. They expire when you close your browser or after your session times out. Without these cookies, the Service cannot function properly.

- **Functional Cookies** — These cookies remember your preferences and settings (such as language or display preferences) to provide a more personalized experience. They may persist between sessions.

### 8.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential session cookies will prevent you from logging in and using the Service.
