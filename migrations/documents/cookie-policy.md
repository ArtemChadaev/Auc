## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your browser, maintain your session, and remember certain preferences. Cookies may be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them).

## 2. Cookies We Use

### Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Platform to function. Without them, core features such as logging in and participating in auctions will not work.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Maintains your active session while you use the Platform | Session (expires on browser close) |
| Authentication token (JWT) | Verifies your identity and keeps you logged in | Persistent (expires per token lifetime) |

### Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance your experience by remembering your choices and preferences.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| User preferences | Stores display and interface preferences | Persistent |
| Language settings | Remembers your selected language | Persistent |

### Cookies We Do NOT Use

- **Advertising or tracking cookies** — We do not serve ads and do not track you for advertising purposes.
- **Third-party analytics cookies** — We do not embed third-party analytics services that place cookies on your device.

## 3. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

- View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
- Block third-party cookies
- Block all cookies
- Clear all cookies when you close the browser

Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions specific to your browser.

> **Important:** If you disable or block **essential cookies**, you will be unable to log in or use the Platform's core functionality, including participating in auctions.

## 4. Data Collected via Cookies

The data collected through our cookies is limited to:

- **Session identifiers** — to maintain your active session
- **Authentication state** — to verify that you are logged in

We do not collect personal data through cookies beyond what is strictly necessary for the operation of the Platform. No browsing behavior, personal profiles, or external tracking data is gathered.

## 5. Third-Party Cookies

Auc does **not** use any third-party cookies. All cookies placed on your device originate solely from the Platform.
